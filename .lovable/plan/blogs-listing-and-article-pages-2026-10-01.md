# Blogs Listing and Article Pages

## What will be built
- Add a new **Blogs** page at `/blogs` showing all articles in a clean, premium grid.
- Add individual article pages at `/blogs/{slug}` with a large cover image, category, reading time, author/date, readable article sections, related articles, and a clear shop call-to-action.
- Add **Blogs** to the main navigation and footer so visitors can discover it easily.
- Include an initial set of useful, store-relevant articles about Rudraksha, Karungali, crystals, malas, and Vastu.

## Design direction
- Match the current ivory, indigo, and antique-gold Nakshatra identity.
- Keep the listing visual and easy to scan, with one featured story followed by compact article cards.
- Make article pages editorial and distraction-free, with comfortable typography and mobile spacing.
- Reuse existing store photography so everything remains visually consistent and fast-loading.

## Technical details
- Store typed blog content in one reusable data file, ready to map to Shopify blog/article objects later.
- Create dedicated TanStack routes for `/blogs` and `/blogs/$slug` with unique SEO and social metadata.
- Add breadcrumb navigation and Article/Blog structured data for search engines.
- Update the sitemap to include the listing and every article URL.
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts, article navigation, and preview health.
