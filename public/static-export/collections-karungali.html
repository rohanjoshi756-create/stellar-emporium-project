Karungali Store — FAQs

How do I check if Karungali is original?+

Original karungali sinks in water, does not release colour when rubbed, and gives a faint natural scent when warmed. Every piece we ship is certified.

Can karungali be worn daily?+

Yes. Keep it away from soap, oil and perfume, and wipe it dry after a bath to preserve the natural finish.
